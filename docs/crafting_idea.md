# M&N's Minecraft Multi Media Mod - 4M

have multiplayer audio/video streaming in minecraft - with an explosive twist :D

# Vision

Create Video Screens and Loudspeakers that work in multiplayer. Players can listen to real time audio and view real time video together. Build a cinema! Build a dance floor!

Place PA Speakers for stereo and directional effects, add mixer and cables to connect it all together.

But start mod development with simpler all-in-one pocket radio and tv.

# Crafting

## road to video screen

To create 4m screen blocks, you need colored quantum dots, blocks of inert black and 4m chassis

unstable liquid alpha channel can be stabilized and turned into colored quantum dots. this is where things might go wrong :)

- a block of water can be transformed into ULAC by hitting it with the Depolarization Hammer
- it becomes unstable immediately: if not scooped with a bucket in a timely manner, it explodes violently and creates a rather nice crater.

This also has an useful sideeffect: the ULAC crystalizes into RGB shards inside the crater which are also needed to create stable oled ingots.

If scooped, the ULAC must be cured to become stable. It can also be poured out to become violent again.

To cure ULAC, it must be put into a furnace. Over time, the ULAC from the bucket will be converted into raw quantum dots, leaving behind the empty bucket.

The RQDs then must be doped to become colored quantum dots, by combining them with rgb shards.

After that you just have to sandwich R,G and B QDs with inert blocks of black and 4m chassis to get 4m video screen blocks (can be toggled between block and pane mode).

Inert Blocks of Black can be created from lava by hitting it with the Depolarization Hammer. Must be harvested with a silktouch pickaxe! Explodes if not silktouch! (or something else?)

The screen is multiblock and as min-max size somehow.


## road to pa cabinet

To create pa cabinet blocks, you need to combine blocks of bass, mid range ingots and treble nuggets

All of the can be created using raw frequency ore (nuggets/ingots/blocks):

Kill Mobs while raw frequency nuggets are within range:
- Bass: Creeper
- Mid: Pillagers?
- Treble: Spider/Skeleton

Each kill converts one nugget/ingot/block

alternatives:

- mine raw freq ore
- convert vanilla ore with some process into RFO

simple start:

- use 4m hammer:
  - block of gold: block is converted to block of bass
  - block of copper: convert to block of mid range
  - block of silver: convert to block of treble.

blocks of bass/mid/treble can be broken down into ingots and nuggets, like gold/copper etc.

Craft pa cabinet with:
- copper
- redstone
- bass
- mid
- treb
- wood panels
- 4m chassis

## 4m audio/video streaming circuit

- base is unmodulated etherial transportation bits.
- hit an obsidian block with the depolarization hammer. it becomes a block of unmodulated etherial transport ore.
- UETO can be smelted into UET blocks/ingots/nuggets
- combine with redstone, any found disc(?) and 4m chassis to create a 4m audio streaming circuit.
- combine 4m ASC with i.e. a ghast tear to create 4m video streaming circuit.
